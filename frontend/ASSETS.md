# Catalogue images

The five equipment images in `public/images/` were generated specifically for this demo with
OpenAI image generation. They are stored locally so the catalogue has no runtime dependency on an
external image service.

Shared prompt direction:

> Photorealistic equipment rental catalogue card image. A single modern machine, centered and
> fully visible on a warm workshop surface, with a softly blurred practical workshop background,
> soft natural side light, charcoal body and muted safety-yellow accents. No people, brand logos,
> text, watermark or price labels.

Individual subjects: cordless drill, random orbital sander, circular saw, high-pressure washer and
carpet extraction cleaner.
